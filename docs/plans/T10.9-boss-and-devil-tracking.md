# T10.9 — Boss and Devil Tracking (Stage 1 Full Sweep)

## Status

Proposed — awaiting explicit approval before implementation.

## Objective

Extend cross-frame entity tracking to cover the remaining stage-1 antagonists that T10.8 deliberately excluded: the **Red Arremer** (devil, flying mid-stage enemy) and the **stage-1 boss** (cyclops / "Big Men", end-of-stage encounter). This closes the stage-1 trace coverage and prepares the vision layer for T12 encounter grammar work on the full first level.

Two preconditions live inside this task:

1. **Resolve the ADR-013 Known Gap "MOG2 scroll reset"** — the stage-1 boss appears only after the camera scrolls, and the MOG2 background model becomes invalid the moment the camera moves. Without scroll-aware reset logic, the boss frames are dominated by background noise.
2. **Calibrate two new `EnemySignature` instances** for the Red Arremer and the cyclops, following the ADR-019 picker pattern established in T10.7.A and T10.8.1.

## Why Now

- **T10.8 leaves stage 1 incomplete** by design (zombi + crow only). T10.9 is the natural follow-up before T12 encounter design touches stage 1.
- **MOG2 scroll reset is blocking-class for any stage 2+ work** — resolving it here unblocks the whole post-scroll surface, not just the cyclops.
- **EntityTracker is already in place** after T10.8.2. Adding two signatures and a scroll-reset hook is incremental work, not a refactor.
- **Picker pattern is proven** by T10.7.A, T10.7.C, T10.7.E, and T10.8.1. Operator workflow is well-understood by now.

## Root Cause Analysis

Two coupled problems prevent stage-1 completeness today:

### Problem 1 — MOG2 background model invalidates on camera scroll

[ADR-013](../adr/ADR-013-opencv-vision-backend.md) introduced MOG2 background subtraction with a fixed history window. The model assumes a static camera. When GNG scrolls horizontally to approach the stage-1 boss, every pixel changes its background classification simultaneously. MOG2 reports the entire frame as foreground until the history window flushes (typically 100+ frames), which masks any real entity motion during and shortly after the scroll.

Currently `gng_vision_config.py` has `reset_on_scroll: bool = True` as a declarative flag, but no code reads it — the reset path is not implemented.

### Problem 2 — Red Arremer and cyclops have no signatures

T10.8.1 calibrated zombi and crow. Red Arremer and cyclops were explicitly out of scope. Without signatures, `EntityTracker` cannot claim their regions and `_entity_type_from_box` falls back to `enemy_a`/`enemy_b` based on area — producing inconsistent classification and no cross-frame continuity.

## Design Decisions

### Decision 1 — Camera scroll detection via frame-pair pixel-shift

Detect horizontal camera scroll by computing the pixel-shift that maximizes correlation between consecutive frames (constrained to ±16 px horizontal, 0 px vertical for stage 1). When the detected shift exceeds a threshold (initially 2 px/frame) for ≥ 3 consecutive frames, the camera is declared "scrolling".

Implementation lives in `packages/vision/scroll_detector.py`. The detector emits `ScrollEvent` records (frame_start, frame_end, total_shift_px). Numeric only — no path references.

**Why pixel-shift correlation and not optical flow?** Correlation is O(window²) per frame with `window=16`, which is fast enough for offline analysis. Dense optical flow (e.g., Farneback) is overkill for a 1-DOF problem and adds an OpenCV-only dependency that constrains future backend swaps.

### Decision 2 — Reset MOG2 on scroll-end, not scroll-start

When a `ScrollEvent` closes, the MOG2 background model is rebuilt from the next `mog2_warmup_frames` frames (currently 50). During the warmup window the vision pipeline emits no tracker output for non-player entities — `ArthurTracker` still operates because Arthur's signature is geometry-based, not background-subtraction-based.

**Why end and not start?** Mid-scroll frames are unusable for entity detection regardless of MOG2 state. Resetting at scroll-end means the new background model captures the new static scene cleanly.

### Decision 3 — Red Arremer signature

The Red Arremer flies erratically (high vertical motion variance), occasionally pauses on platforms, and throws a projectile arc. Geometric signature:

- Height: larger than zombi/crow, smaller than cyclops. Calibrated via picker.
- center_y range: covers both ground perches and flying altitudes.
- `aspect_ratio`: wings extended → wider than tall; perched → square-ish.
- `max_frame_jump_px`: much higher than zombi (estimate 40–50 px/frame for swoop), calibrated empirically.
- `gap_tolerance_frames`: 5 (covers brief overlaps with Arthur during combat).

### Decision 4 — Cyclops signature

The cyclops is the largest sprite on stage 1 — tall and wide, stationary or short-step. Geometric signature:

- Height: substantially larger than any prior enemy.
- center_y range: ground level only.
- `aspect_ratio`: vertical bias.
- `max_frame_jump_px`: low (8–12 px/frame), calibrated empirically.
- `gap_tolerance_frames`: 3.

Two cyclopes may be on screen simultaneously in the standard stage-1 boss layout — `EntityTracker`'s multi-entity support (T10.8.2) handles this without changes.

### Decision 5 — Capture strategy: short complementary run for boss frames

`run_t10_4_01` may not contain a complete stage-1 boss encounter (the user might have died or quit before reaching it). A short complementary capture (`run_t10_9_boss`) is performed with the user driving Arthur to the boss encounter intentionally, then dying once to avoid extending beyond stage 1.

The capture exists only for signature calibration. The regenerated `gng_trace.json` for downstream consumers continues to use the same primary run (or a merged dataset) per T10.8.5 conventions.

### Decision 6 — Scope: stage 1 full sweep, nothing beyond

Stage 2+ entities, the death scene, and the inter-stage transition remain out of scope. The scroll-reset machinery enables future stage-2 work but T10.9 itself stops at the end of stage 1.

## Risks

- **R1 — Scroll detection false positives during Arthur's mid-air motion**: a large player sprite can dominate frame correlation. Mitigation: mask the player region (using `ArthurTracker.find_arthur`) before computing pixel-shift correlation.
- **R2 — MOG2 warmup window misses early boss spawn**: if the cyclops appears within the first 50 post-scroll frames, it is invisible. Mitigation: tune `mog2_warmup_frames` empirically; consider falling back to direct frame-diff during warmup for known signature types only.
- **R3 — Red Arremer overlaps Arthur's signature bounds**: a perched Arremer at ground level may match Arthur's `center_y_min/max`. Mitigation: greedy assignment by `spawn_frame` ascending (Arthur spawns first) plus per-signature `max_frame_jump_px` rejects cross-claims.
- **R4 — Two cyclopes overlap visually**: standard stage-1 boss layout has two cyclopes near each other; they may briefly merge into one FrameDiffer region. Mitigation: `gap_tolerance_frames` bridges the merge; if persistent, document as an accepted limitation.
- **R5 — Scroll detector cost on long runs**: pixel-shift correlation per frame across a multi-thousand-frame run is non-trivial. Mitigation: short window (±16 px) keeps cost bounded; cache results in the per-frame stat object.

## Dependency Order

T10.9 inserts after T10.8 and before T11.2.

Rationale:

- T10.9 relies on the `EntityTracker` and `EnemySignature` infrastructure introduced by T10.8.2.
- T10.9 resolves the ADR-013 MOG2 scroll-reset Known Gap, which removes a precondition for any future stage-2+ tracking work.
- T11.2 episode extraction benefits from a stage-1-complete trace (zombi + crow + devil + boss) rather than a partial one.

## Exit Criteria

T10.9 is complete when:

1. `ScrollDetector` exists, detects horizontal camera scrolls, and emits `ScrollEvent` records.
2. The vision pipeline resets the MOG2 background model at the end of each `ScrollEvent` and re-warms over the configured warmup window.
3. `EnemySignature` for Red Arremer (entity_type `enemy_c`) and cyclops (entity_type `boss_a`) are calibrated and committed to `specs/calibration/gng_enemy_signatures.yaml`.
4. Regenerated `gng_trace.json` contains coherent per-entity lifecycle for the boss encounter: at least one cyclops track with duration ≥ 60 frames; at least one Red Arremer track with duration ≥ 20 frames (when an Arremer appears in the run).
5. All T10.8 validation gates continue to pass on the regenerated trace.
6. ADR-022 is published; ADR-013 "MOG2 scroll reset" Known Gap is removed.
7. All Python + TypeScript tests pass with no regressions.
