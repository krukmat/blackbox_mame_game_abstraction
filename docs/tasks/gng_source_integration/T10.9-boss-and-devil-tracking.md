# T10.9 — Boss and Devil Tracking (Stage 1 Full Sweep)

## Status

🔲 Planned — awaiting explicit approval before implementation.

## Objective

Extend cross-frame entity tracking to the **Red Arremer** (devil, flying mid-stage enemy) and the **stage-1 boss** (cyclops / "Big Men"), closing stage-1 trace coverage. Two preconditions are absorbed into this task: resolve the ADR-013 "MOG2 scroll reset" Known Gap (so the post-scroll boss frames are usable) and calibrate two new `EnemySignature` instances via the ADR-019 picker pattern.

## Scope

- Implement `packages/vision/scroll_detector.py` with `ScrollDetector` + `ScrollEvent` (ADR-022).
- Wire MOG2 reset on `ScrollEvent` end through `packages/vision/frame_differ.py` (or pipeline orchestrator); honor `gng_vision_config.reset_on_scroll`.
- Calibrate `EnemySignature` for Red Arremer (`enemy_c`) and cyclops (`boss_a`) via picker; append to `specs/calibration/gng_enemy_signatures.yaml`.
- If `run_t10_4_01` lacks coherent boss footage, perform a short complementary capture (`run_t10_9_boss`) targeting the boss encounter.
- Regenerate `specs/traces/gng_trace.json` and verify against extended quality gates.
- Publish ADR-022; remove the ADR-013 "MOG2 scroll reset" Known Gap.

## Out of Scope

- Stage 2+ scrolling sections (volcano, jail, etc.) — the scroll-aware machinery enables them, but T10.9 does not produce stage-2 traces.
- Boss behavior modelling (attack patterns, phase transitions, HP) — that is T12 encounter grammar territory.
- Boss projectiles (lances thrown by cyclops/devil) — projectile work is covered by the T10.7.E pattern; reuse if needed but do not extend it here.
- Vertical scroll handling (not present in GNG).
- ROM-state introspection (forbidden by clean-room policy).
- Updates to RN PhysicsSystem — boss physics are not yet consumed downstream.

## Dependencies

- ✅ T10.8.2 — `EntityTracker` and `EntitySignature` infrastructure.
- ✅ T10.8.5 — clean regenerated trace as baseline; T10.9 regenerates on top of T10.8's pipeline state.
- ✅ T10.8.6 — ADR-013 enemy-continuity Known Gap removed; T10.9 removes the scroll-reset one.

## Blocks

- Any future stage 2+ tracking work.
- T12.3 encounter grammar for stage 1 (requires complete enemy roster).

## Reasoning Grade

`High`

Reasoning basis:
- Cross-cutting change in the vision pipeline (new module + MOG2 lifecycle hook).
- Two new signature calibrations with picker-based human validation.
- Pipeline ordering invariant (ArthurTracker → ScrollDetector → MOG2 → EntityTracker) must be enforced and tested.
- Boss frame capture may require a fresh MAME session, which introduces operator coordination cost.

## Effort Grade

`L`

## Recommended Model

`Sonnet`

## Acceptance Criteria

1. `packages/vision/scroll_detector.py` exists with `ScrollDetector`, `ScrollEvent`, and unit tests covering: pure-horizontal scroll, no-scroll stillness, player-mask exclusion, debounce of brief pauses.
2. MOG2 background model is rebuilt at the end of each `ScrollEvent`; non-player tracker output is suppressed for `mog2_warmup_frames` frames after reset.
3. `gng_vision_config.reset_on_scroll` is actually read and applied by the pipeline; setting it to `False` reverts to ADR-013 behavior.
4. `specs/calibration/gng_enemy_signatures.yaml` contains calibrated bounds for `enemy_c` (Red Arremer) and `boss_a` (cyclops) with `calibration_source`, `sample_count`, and `calibration_method: enemy_signature_picker`.
5. Operator has accepted ≥ 3 Red Arremer candidate clusters and ≥ 3 cyclops candidate clusters.
6. Regenerated `gng_trace.json` contains, for the boss-encounter section:
   - ≥ 1 cyclops track with duration ≥ 60 frames.
   - ≥ 1 Red Arremer track with duration ≥ 20 frames (only required if an Arremer appears in the run).
   - No spurious tracker output during the MOG2 warmup window.
7. All T10.8 validation gates continue to pass on the regenerated trace.
8. ADR-022 is published; ADR-013 "MOG2 scroll reset" Known Gap is removed from `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, and the ADR-013 document.
9. Full Python + TypeScript test suites pass with no regressions.
10. Per CLAUDE.md, **no commit if tests are broken**.

## Risks

- **R1 — Scroll detector false positives on Arthur motion**: mitigated by player masking before correlation.
- **R2 — MOG2 warmup hides early boss spawn**: tunable `mog2_warmup_frames`; consider direct frame-diff fallback during warmup for known signatures only.
- **R3 — Red Arremer overlaps Arthur signature bounds**: greedy `EntityTracker` ordering by `spawn_frame` ascending favors longer-lived tracks (Arthur).
- **R4 — Two cyclopes overlap visually**: `gap_tolerance_frames` bridges merges; persistent merges accepted as a documented limitation.
- **R5 — Boss footage unavailable in existing evidence**: short complementary capture (`run_t10_9_boss`) is the documented fallback.
