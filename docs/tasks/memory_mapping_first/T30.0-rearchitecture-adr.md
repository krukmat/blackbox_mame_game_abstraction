## Why subdivided

The original T30.0 mixed one judgement-heavy part (drafting the ADR decision and choosing
what to demote/deprecate) with a lot of mechanical propagation across 4+ canonical docs. The
judgement was moved into planning (the content is embedded verbatim below in the subtasks), so
the remaining work is mechanical and safe for a thinking-off model with a small blast radius
per run.
