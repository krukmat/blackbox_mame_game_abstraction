# T20.9 — VLM-Assisted Archetype + Mechanic Labeling (ADR-027)

## Objective
Use a vision-language model to propose entity archetypes and candidate mechanics from short
private clips, with the human bulk-confirming a short list instead of accepting per-candidate
picks one at a time.

## Scope
- A workflow that samples short private clips per tracked entity (from T20.8 / T20.2 traces)
  and asks a VLM to propose an abstract archetype ("small ground walker", "flying diver",
  "large boss") and candidate behaviors — abstract descriptors only, never copyright names.
- Output a structured proposal table; human bulk-confirms/edits; confirmed archetypes feed
  `specs/entities/` archetypes and asset-recipe roles.
- Clean-room guard: clips and any image bytes stay private; only the confirmed abstract
  descriptors (strings/numbers) cross to public specs. No VLM-returned proper nouns allowed.
- Author `docs/adr/ADR-027-vlm-assisted-archetype-labeling.md`: model proposes, human confirms;
  only abstract descriptors are published; originality contract (ADR-007) still applies.

## Out of scope
- Generating art (still gated by ADR-007 human review).
- Replacing behavioral validation (ADR-008).

## Dependencies
T20.2, T20.4 (and benefits from T20.8 identities).

## Reasoning grade
High — clean-room risk (VLM could surface copyright identity); contract must strip it.

## Effort grade
Medium.

## Acceptance criteria
- VLM proposals are abstract; a guard rejects any copyright proper noun before publication.
- Human confirmation is bulk (one review for many entities), not per-candidate.
- Confirmed archetypes land in public specs with no private paths/image bytes.
- ADR-027 authored; indices/vault updated; ADR-007 originality contract referenced.

## Recommended model
Opus.
