# T20.7 — Optical-Flow Scroll Estimation + Compensation (ADR-025 pt.1)

## Objective
Detect and compensate camera scroll automatically via global-motion (optical-flow) estimation,
replacing the MOG2 scroll-reset hack (ADR-022) and enabling detection on all stages, not just
stage 1.

## Scope
- A global-motion estimator (e.g., dense/sparse optical flow or phase correlation on the
  non-HUD region) that outputs per-frame camera displacement (dx, dy).
- Compensate frames into a stabilized reference before background subtraction / diffing, so
  the background model is not invalidated by scroll.
- Author `docs/adr/ADR-025-modern-vision-backend.md` (part 1: scroll compensation); state how
  it supersedes the ADR-022 scroll-reset Known Gap.

## Out of scope
- Tracking-by-detection (T20.8).
- Memory tap (T20.5).

## Dependencies
T20.0 (informs whether vision is primary or hardening).

## Reasoning grade
High — vision algorithm correctness; clean-room (numbers-only) preserved.

## Effort grade
Medium-High.

## Acceptance criteria
- Estimated camera displacement matches a synthetic scroll fixture within tolerance.
- Stationary background entities remain detectable across a scroll boundary (no full reset).
- ADR-025 (pt.1) authored; ADR-022 Known Gap updated/closed accordingly.
- Output remains numbers-only; no frame paths cross the boundary (ADR-006).

## Recommended model
Opus.
